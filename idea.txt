New "Schedule" menu item appears in the tray menu with a checkmark indicator when a schedule is active.
How it works:
    Click Schedule → opens a dialog with:
    A checkbox to Enable schedule
    Start time (HH : MM spinboxes, 24-hour)
    End time (HH : MM spinboxes, 24-hour)
    OK / Cancel buttons
When enabled, reminders only fire if the current time falls within the window
Supports overnight ranges too (e.g. 22:00 – 06:00)
Settings are persisted to settings.json and restored on next launch
Restore Default also resets the schedule (disabled, 09:00–18:00)
The tray menu item shows a checkmark (✓) whenever the schedule is currently enabled
